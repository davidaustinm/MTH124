    MTH 124

    Activity 24.

    Suppose the total cost in dollars to produce x items is given by the function \(C(x)\text{,}\) and that \(AV_{[100,200]} = 24.04\text{.}\)
    🔗

    (a)

    What are the units of the average rate of change?
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    What does \(AV_{[100,200]} = 24.04\) mean in context?
    🔗
    🔗

    (c)

    If \(AV_{[100,200]} = 24.04\) and \(AV_{[200,300]}=36.04\text{,}\) what can you say about \(C(x)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    Activity 25.

    Suppose an object is thrown upward with initial velocity of 32 feet per second from a height of 50 feet. The height of the object t seconds after it is thrown is given by a function \(h(t)\text{.}\)
    🔗

    (a)

    If \(AV_{[0,2]} = 0\text{,}\) what are the units, and what does that mean in context?
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    If \(AV_{[2,4]} = -64\text{,}\) what are the units, and what does that mean in context?
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    Activity 26.

    There is a specific breed of bacteria whose global population after \(t\) months is given by the function \(p(t) = 4t^5 + 4t^2+4240.\)
    🔗

    (a)

    If the average rate of change of \(p(t)\) on the interval from 0 to 6 is 5208, what does 5208 mean in context (include units!)?
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    What is \(AV_{[6,12]}\text{?}\) Include units.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    Activity 27.

    The velocity of a skydiver is given by a function \(v(t)\text{,}\) where \(v(t)\) is in feet per second and \(t\) is in seconds.
    🔗

    (a)

    What would be the units or the average rate of change of \(v(t)\text{?}\)
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    If \(AV_{[0,10]}=147\text{,}\) what would that mean in context? Use the units to help you!
    🔗
    🔗

    (c)

    The table below gives the velocity \(v(t)\) in 10 second intervals. Velocity is measured in feet per second (fps).
    🔗
    Time in seconds 0 10 20 30 40 50 60
    Velocity in fps 0 147 171 175 175.8 176 176
    Find \(AV_{[50,60]}\text{.}\) What does it mean about the skydiver?
    🔗
